64×64 texture workbench · no cloud · no nonsense

Canvas · 64 × 64

Grid on

Draw with your finger or S Pen. Each stroke paints crisp individual pixels. Grid lines are only a guide and never appear in your PNG.

Layers / stack

Layers stack from bottom to top. Select a layer to draw on it. Copy/Paste duplicates a layer; move it by coordinates or one pixel at a time. Off-canvas pixels are clipped, not deleted.

Local texture generator

Describe a tiny game texture. Runs entirely in your browser with procedural generation—no account, server, or downloads.

Enter a material or style, then generate. Try: stone, grass, water, wood, metal, lava, ice, sand, brick, alien, fabric.

Export / Import

Export PNG always downloads an exact 64 × 64 image without grid lines or smoothing. Save project keeps the editable pixels.
